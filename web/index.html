ARTFORGE / FIELD GUIDE

BREAK SOMETHING
BEAUTIFUL.

  1. Bring your own signal. Drop a photo, screenshot, or video. Or generate a byte dump, circuit-board diagram, or boot-screen still from your seed. MP4 decoding depends on your browser's codec support. Files stay on your device.
  2. Find the damage. Choose a mutation, enable Stack presets to combine looks, push the sliders, or press M. Shared controls use the last selected preset. The seed lets you repeat a result.
  3. Make it move. Press Space. Set loop in/out points. Popup replicas can drift, orbit, or bounce. Write your own popup messages or import an image to replicate. Recipes save custom messages; reimport your custom overlay image separately. Temporal echo blends previous frames while playing.
  4. Feed it sound. Import audio or use a video's soundtrack. Enable reactivity to drive tearing and channel separation. Added audio replaces the video's audio.
  5. Keep what you make. Export a PNG or record a loop in real time. Recordings include the selected soundtrack when browser support allows. Stay on this tab while recording.

PNG export is capped at 2560px on the longest edge. Preview and recordings are capped at 960px for responsive effects. Large media stays local; very large images may exceed your browser's memory.

Recipes work in the updated terminal tool: python3 artforge.py forge photo.png --recipe recipe.json. Image effects share the same recipe engine; video uses FFmpeg interpretations of those controls. Fonts and feedback can differ.
